# Driftwood Seed Pitch Deck — Approach

## Output
`Driftwood_Seed_Pitch_Deck.pptx` — 11 slides, 16:9, built directly with `python-pptx` (no templating tool or slide-building skill used, per instructions).

## Structure (kept intentionally tight — angels skim)
1. Title / one-line positioning + the ask teased
2. Problem — freight, warehousing, returns as the three pain points
3. Solution — 3-step fulfillment flow + pricing model (per-item fee + monthly storage fee)
4. Traction — 42 makers, 15% MoM x4 months, $340 ARPU, current MRR, a derived 4-month growth bar chart
5. Business model / unit economics — pricing breakdown + today's blended numbers (MRR, ARR run-rate)
6. Market — sizing narrative + a 3-row positioning table (consumer carriers vs. enterprise 3PLs vs. Driftwood)
7. Why now — three tailwinds
8. The plan — path from 42 to 150 makers in 12 months, with a projected bar chart and the operational levers
9. Why us — reframed as proof-of-model/execution evidence (see assumptions)
10. The ask — $1.2M, use of funds broken into 3 buckets
11. Close — contact placeholder

## Key numbers used (as given)
- 42 makers, 15% MoM growth for 4 months, $340/mo ARPU -> current MRR ~= $14.3K, ARR run-rate ~= $171K.
- Raise: $1.2M seed; goal: 150 makers in 12 months (~3.6x), funding 2 ops hires.

## Assumptions / judgment calls made (not provided in the brief — flagged for founder to revise)
- **No team/founder info was given.** I did not invent names, titles, or bios. Slide 9 was repurposed from a generic "Team" slide into a "Why Us" slide built entirely from the traction/execution evidence already in hand, rather than fabricating credentials. **The founder should swap in a real founder/team slide before sending.**
- **Historical maker-count chart (slide 4)** — only the end value (42) and growth rate (15% MoM) were given; the trailing month-by-month counts (24 -> 28 -> 36 -> 42) were back-calculated from those two figures to make the growth tangible, not sourced from actual data.
- **12-month maker projection (slide 8)** — 150 makers in 12 months implies ~10.6% MoM growth (lower than the trailing 15%, a reasonable deceleration assumption as volume scales); the intermediate milestones (58/80/110) are an illustrative, roughly-exponential path, not a provided plan.
- **Use-of-funds split (slide 10)** — the brief specified 2 ops hires but not a dollar allocation. I split the $1.2M into three plausible buckets (ops hires, warehouse/carrier network expansion, working capital/runway) to make the ask concrete. The founder should replace these with real budgeted figures.
- **Market sizing (slide 6)** — described qualitatively ("$100B+ market," "tens of thousands of makers") rather than with a precise TAM/SAM figure, since no market research was provided and a fabricated precise number would be misleading in a diligence setting.
- Contact email/domain on the closing slide is a placeholder (`hello@driftwood.co`) — swap for the real one.

## Design
Warm, workshop-neutral palette (driftwood brown, ink, sand, warm paper) to visually nod to the furniture-maker niche without looking like a generic template. Dark cover/ask/close bookends, light content slides in between for scan-ability.

# Driftwood deck - build decisions

Theme: warm-startup (FFFBF5 / B5481A / F2994A, Verdana font). Picked because this is an early pitch deck that should read as energetic and approachable rather than corporate, and the warm palette fits a maker/craft brand.

Detail level: built tight per the request - 9 slides (title, problem, solution, traction KPIs, business model, why now, 12-month plan KPIs, ask, closing). No team slide and no competitive landscape slide, since no inputs existed for either. No TAM/SAM figures were invented.

Derived numbers (computed from the user's own figures, not independently sourced):
- Current MRR ~$14.3K = 42 makers x $340/mo.
- Projected MRR at the 150-maker target ~$51K/mo = 150 x $340, assuming flat ARPM.
- Growth rate needed to hit 150 makers in 12 months ~11%/mo, shown next to the actual 15%/mo pace to make the point that the target is conservative given current traction.

No inconsistencies were found in the source numbers; the 42-maker count, 15% MoM growth, and 150-maker/12-month goal are mutually consistent.

Assumption: no contact info was given, so the closing slide's optional contact line was left blank rather than invented.

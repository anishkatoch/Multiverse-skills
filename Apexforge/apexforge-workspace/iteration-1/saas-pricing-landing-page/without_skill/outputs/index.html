Questions, answered plainly

Nothing breaks and nothing charges automatically. Your workflows pause until you pick a plan, and we'll email you three days before the trial ends so there are no surprises. Your data and workflow configurations stay saved for 30 days either way.

Yes, anytime from your billing settings. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the remainder of your cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period, so you keep what you've already paid for.

One run is one completed trip through a workflow - for example, "new form submission → create task → notify channel" counts as a single run, even though it touches three apps. Failed or retried steps within the same trigger don't count twice.

Annual billing saves 20% on every plan, charged as one upfront payment. Registered nonprofits and student-run teams get an additional 30% off on top of that - reach out to our team with proof of status and we'll apply it manually.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and workflows are only visible to members you've explicitly added to your workspace. Business plans add role-based permissions and a full audit log so you can see exactly who changed what, and when.

You don't need to re-build anything. Upgrading to Pro or Business unlocks the rest of our integration catalog instantly, and any workflows you've already built keep running - you're just adding new steps to them.
